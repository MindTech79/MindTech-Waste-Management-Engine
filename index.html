Privacy Notice · POPIA Compliance

Privacy Notice & POPIA Compliance

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What We Collect

When you submit a waste management entry, we collect: your name, phone number, email address, area/community, GPS coordinates (if you drop a pin), waste type, volume, and any community safety contacts you provide (WhatsApp group, security company).

2. Why We Collect It

This data is used to:

  • Issue a quantum-verified certificate of collection.
  • Anchor the certificate to the PELO master ledger.
  • Provide an immutable audit trail for regulators, auditors, and the community.
  • Coordinate waste collection and recycling logistics.

3. Where It Is Stored

All data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, in the US-East (Oregon) region. Google Cloud is a third-party operator. Data is not sold, and it is not used to train any AI model.

4. Cross-Border Transfer (POPIA Section 72)

Because the data is stored in the United States, this constitutes a cross-border transfer of personal information. The transfer relies on the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which provides binding data protection obligations consistent with POPIA.

5. How Long We Keep It

Data is retained for 365 days for audit and compliance purposes, after which it is anonymised or deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@mindtech.industries.

7. Consent

By submitting an entry, you consent to your data being stored and processed as described in this notice. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

8. Security

All data is signed with a quantum-verified RSA-PSS-SHA256 signature and anchored to an immutable ledger. The certificate hash is recomputable from the record using SHA-256 over the canonical JSON serialisation.
